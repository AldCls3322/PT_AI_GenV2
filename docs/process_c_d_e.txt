PROCESS C — INCIDENT ESCALATION
Banorte Internal Knowledge Base — Version 1.0

WHAT QUALIFIES AS AN INCIDENT
──────────────────────────────
- Unauthorized transactions or suspected card fraud.
- Account access issues (locked accounts, compromised credentials).
- ATM malfunction that deducted funds without dispensing cash.
- Unauthorized standing orders or direct debits.
- Identity theft or phishing attack.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS FOR THE CLIENT
  1. Block the card immediately via app → "Block Card" (instant, 24/7).
  2. Call the fraud hotline: 800-226-6783, Option 3 (available 24/7).
  3. Do NOT share OTP codes, passwords, or full card numbers with anyone.

INCIDENT LOGGING
  - Every incident receives a folio number (e.g., INC-2024-0001).
  - Client receives SMS and email confirmation within 15 minutes.
  - Provisional credit may be applied within 24 hours for qualifying disputes.

ESCALATION LEVELS
  Level 1 — First contact resolution (0–2 hours)
  Level 2 — Operations specialist (2–4 hours)
  Level 3 — Fraud investigation team (4–24 hours)
  Level 4 — Legal / Regulatory — for cases involving law enforcement

SLA: Critical incidents resolved within 4 hours; investigation closed within 10 business days.
Channel: Phone 800-226-6783, branch, app secure message.

PROCESS D — CUSTOMER DATA UPDATE
Banorte Internal Knowledge Base — Version 1.0

UPDATABLE DATA
──────────────
- Home address (domicilio fiscal y de correspondencia)
- Phone number (móvil and landline)
- Email address
- RFC / CURP (requires supporting documentation)
- Beneficiaries

REQUIREMENTS BY DATA TYPE
  Address update:
    - Recent utility bill (not older than 3 months) or official lease agreement.
    - Official government-issued ID.
    - Can be done in-branch or via app for minor updates.

  Phone / Email update:
    - Verify identity with OTP sent to current registered number.
    - Update in app: Profile → Personal Data → Contact Info.

  RFC / CURP update:
    - SAT-issued RFC document or CURP certificate.
    - Must be processed in-branch — cannot be done remotely.

  Beneficiary update:
    - Official ID of new beneficiary.
    - In-branch only. Notarized document if beneficiary is a minor.

PROCESSING TIME: 1–3 business days for most updates; up to 3 business days for RFC/CURP.
Responsible team: KYC & Compliance. Priority: Medium.

PROCESS E — INTERNAL COMPLAINT MANAGEMENT
Banorte Internal Knowledge Base — Version 1.0

CLIENT RIGHTS
─────────────
All Banorte clients have the right to file a formal complaint (queja) or
claim (reclamación) under CONDUSEF regulations (Comisión Nacional para la
Protección y Defensa de los Usuarios de Servicios Financieros).

COMPLAINT SUBMISSION CHANNELS
  - Online: banorte.com → "Atención al Cliente" → "Quejas y Reclamaciones"
  - App: Help → File a Complaint
  - Branch: Ask for the Complaints & Quality desk
  - Email: quejas@banorte.com
  - CONDUSEF direct: 800-999-8080 or condusef.gob.mx

COMPLAINT PROCESS
  1. Submission: Client provides written or verbal description.
  2. Acknowledgment: Folio assigned within 2 business hours.
  3. Investigation: Quality & Complaints unit reviews the case.
  4. Resolution: Written response sent within 5 business days.
  5. Appeal: If unsatisfied, client may escalate to CONDUSEF (20-day window).

COMPENSATIONS
  - If complaint is upheld: fee reversal, corrected credit bureau report, or monetary compensation depending on the case.
  - Banorte does not charge any fee to process complaints.

SLA: 5 business days for standard complaints; 20 business days for complex cases.
Responsible team: Quality & Complaints Unit. Priority: High.
